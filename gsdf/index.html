Medical display calibration

GSDF Calibration & Compliance Service

Ensure your medical imaging app meets FDA requirements for display accuracy. Our GSDF Calibration & Compliance Service provides validated luminance testing, DICOM PS 3.14 curve plotting, and complete documentation to support your 510(k) submission.

01

GSDF Curve Calibration

We generate and validate the Grayscale Standard Display Function (GSDF) for your app using test patterns and luminance measurements. All results are charted and compared against DICOM PS 3.14 expectations.

02

Mobile Display QA (iOS & iPadOS)

Device-specific testing for Apple displays using calibrated test environments. We measure rendering behavior of your DICOM viewer across brightness ranges and hardware configurations (e.g. iPhone 15 Pro, iPad Air M2).

03

Luminance & JND Testing

Precise luminance readings across grayscale levels allow us to map Just Noticeable Difference (JND) indices. This ensures consistent diagnostic image interpretation in variable lighting conditions.

04

Full Compliance Documentation

Includes test procedures, luminance plots, deviation analysis, and pass/fail assessments. Delivered as a 510(k)-ready PDF with all technical data.
