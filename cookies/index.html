Your privacy

Cookies & local storage

Last updated 7 October 2026.

No advertising. No analytics.

This website does not set analytics or advertising cookies, use tracking pixels, or embed third-party video players. Our videos and images are hosted with the site. Fonts come from your device.

What we save on your device

After you accept or reject optional cookies, we save that choice in your browser’s local storage. Local storage is a browser feature, separate from HTTP cookies. This record stays on your device and is not sent to MEDIWARE.

Name
mediware-cookie-choice
Purpose
Remember your privacy choice so the notice does not reappear on every page.
Contents
Your selection, the policy version, and an expiry date. No visitor identifier.
Duration
180 days. An expired record is removed the next time you visit.
Provider
MEDIWARE CY, stored only in your browser.

Your choice

Accept all and Reject optional are equally available. No optional cookies are currently used, so neither choice enables tracking. Acceptance does not authorize any future analytics or advertising service. If optional services are introduced, the notice and settings will need to describe them and ask for a new choice before they load.

Open Cookie settings in the footer to change your selection or clear your saved preference. Clearing it withdraws your saved choice and shows the notice again. If your browser blocks local storage, your selection still dismisses the notice for the current page, but cannot be remembered for later visits.

External sites and hosting

App Store and social links take you to external websites, which have their own privacy and cookie policies. GitHub Pages logs visitor IP addresses for security when it hosts this site. Cookie choices do not disable those hosting security logs. Read GitHub’s hosting information.

Contact

For questions about privacy, email legal@mediware.cy. See the privacy policy for wider information about MEDIWARE’s services.
